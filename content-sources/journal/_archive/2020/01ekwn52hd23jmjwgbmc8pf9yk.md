[Freedom Isn't Free](https://logicmag.io/failure/freedom-isnt-free/)

> [To make information free, it is necessary to make the production of information free: a society where basic needs are taken care of and there is no need to be paid to produce.]

> [Free information is incompatible with corporations profiting off of the commidification of information.]
