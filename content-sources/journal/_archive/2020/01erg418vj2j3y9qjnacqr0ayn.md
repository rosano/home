[How Substack Became Milquetoast](https://nintil.com/substack-milquetoast)

> [Blogging has capped downside and unlimited upside. If create something mediocre, nobody talks about it. If it resonates, everyone thinks your a genius. Having more content gives you more options for someone to develop an interest in your work and follow what you're doing.]

> [Better for authors to think persistently and write occasionally instead of the other way around.]

> [A book that took 4 years to write and 10 hours to read is a 3500x return on time investment.]

> [Nothing great will ever be written on Substack because the dynamics of monthly payments force you to publish at  faster schedules and leave you with less space to think.]

> [Each day you work endures is a closer step towards longevity and the possibility that it will become immortal.]
