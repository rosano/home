> [A block of text creates a certain texture based on the thickness and evenness of letter stroke, the serifs.]

> [A font's point size is based on the difference between the tallest ascender (like the tip of a 'b') and the lowest descender (like the tail of a 'p').]

> [Body copy tends to look better with a leading / line-height of 120% to 140% of a type's size (1.2em to 1.4em).]

> [Similar character widths make for better pairings.]

> [Typefaces are built for specific mediums such as print or screen.]

> [If you seperate paragraphs with space, a full line break is usually too much, better to have 0.8em.]
