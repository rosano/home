> [Multiple examples and analogies, as opposed to just one, help illuminate variability in the application of a concept.]

> [*Negative transfer* refers to memorizing something in the wrong context; almost like functional fixedness. *Positive transfer* refers to the successful application of a concept to a new context.]

> [People can misinterpret short-term failure as an indicator that they don't belong. Help them re-frame it as simply setbacks and nothing more, provide models of others who felt similarly in their situation.]

> [Mitigate stereotype threat by emphasizing how people have multiple identities and focusing on the facets that are in-group.]

> [Feeling a social connection or group belonging increases persistence on challenging tasks.]
