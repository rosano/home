[Structured distraction: how to make the most of your breaks at work](https://nesslabs.com/structured-distraction-breaks-at-work)

> [Think about the kind of break you take: a 'movement break' do do something physical with your body; a 'mental break' to meditate or let the mind wander; a 'social break' to connect with others; a 'nature break' to get some sun or be around trees; a creative break to be artful in a non-goal oriented way.]
