[Goal Setting: A Scientific Guide to Setting and Achieving Goals](https://jamesclear.com/goal-setting)

> [Goal setting is as much about the pain and sacrifices you are willing to endure as the desired outcome.]

> [Goals determine the destination, systems determine your progress.]

> [Increase the likelihood of sticking to your goals by planning specifically when, where, and how you will do it.]

> [Hook new plans before or after existing habits.]

> [Opt in while you're feeling motivated so that when the time comes you will have to justify opting out.]
