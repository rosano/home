> What you get, the cobbling together of hundreds of thousands of prior conversations, is a kind of conversational purée. Made of human parts, but less than a human sum. Users *are*, in effect, chatting with a kind of purée of real people-the *ghosts* of real people, at any rate: the echoes of conversations past.
