> To a large extent I don't want to be influenced by data when I chose what to work on. It's very easy to get sucked into "number go up" and optimize for numbers rather than experience.

> Analytics are a proxy. They can give you the feeling that you have all the data you need to make decisions. But you can easily end up in a bubble of indirect perspective about your product, and miss the first-hand knowledge that comes from building for yourself and listening to users.
