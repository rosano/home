[Bangalore India hospital flow](https://sive.rs/inflow)

> I love watching how they drive in India. It’s not orderly. **But neither is water flowing in a stream.** It flows around obstacles. Everything is opportunistic, selfishly taking the effective path, exploiting available gaps. It’s natural.

> I showed up at 5am, checked in for the 6am surgery, changed into the gown, and waited. Nobody came. I asked a few times, but nobody knew where he was. After four hours of waiting, I walked out and made an appointment with a different surgeon, and that went great.
