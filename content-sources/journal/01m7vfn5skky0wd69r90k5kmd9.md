[Nominology](https://messymatters.com/nominology/)

> [Criteria for good names:
> - *Evocativity*: hints at what it names
> - *Brevity*: shorter is better
> - *Greppability*: doesn't conflict with common words
> - *Googlability*: unique enough and with an available domain name
> - *Pronounceability*: simple to say when reading
> - *Spellability*: simple to spell when hearing
> - *Verbability*: can be turned into a verb]
