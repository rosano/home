> We once thought humans were unique for having a language with syntactical rules, but this isn't so; we once thought humans were unique for using tools, but this isn't so; we once thought humans were unique for being able to do mathematics, and now we can barely imagine being able to do what our calculators can.

> each new step towards AI, rather than producing something which everyone agrees is real intelligence, merely reveals what real intelligence is *not*.
